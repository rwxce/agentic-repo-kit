## Copy rules

Each rule earned its place by being broken; keep one before/after per rule.

1. **Every section names the doubt it answers.**
   > ~~"<feature description>"~~ → "<the visitor's sentence, answered>"
2. **Nothing in the past tense that hasn't happened.** On a first visit the product has made
   nothing; prefer an imperative the visitor can verify.
3. **No internal jargon before it is taught.** Introduce a term once, then use the short form.
4. **Don't say twice what the UI already implies.**
